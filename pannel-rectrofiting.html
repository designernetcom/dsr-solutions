CNC Machine Retrofitting Services

About CNC Retrofitting

CNC (Computer Numerical Control) machine retrofitting services involve upgrading or modernizing existing CNC machines to enhance their performance, precision, and efficiency without the need for buying an entirely new machine. These services are widely sought after by manufacturing companies looking to extend the life of their existing equipment while gaining access to modern control technology.

Our Retrofitting Services

What is CNC Machine Retrofitting?

CNC retrofitting is the process of replacing outdated or obsolete CNC controls, drives, motors, or other machine components with modern equivalents to improve functionality, reliability, and productivity.

Benefits of Retrofitting

  • Cost-Effective - Far cheaper than buying a new machine.
  • Improved Performance - Faster cycle times, better accuracy.
  • Increased Reliability - New components reduce downtime and maintenance.
  • Better Support & Parts Availability - Modern systems are easier to maintain and source parts for.
  • Extended Machine Life - Makes older machines productive again.
  • Integration with Modern Systems - Includes networking, remote monitoring, and automation capabilities.

01. Control Panel Upgrade

Replace outdated controllers with advanced CNC systems like FANUC, Siemens, or Mach3/4.

02. Servo & Drive Replacement

Install high-performance servos and drives to boost performance and reliability.

03. Wiring & Electrical Overhaul

Complete rewiring and new control cabinets to meet today's electrical standards.

04.Mechanical Component Revamp

Ball screw replacement, linear guides upgrades, and spindle reconditioning.

05.Software Integration

CAD/CAM software setup, touchscreen UI, remote monitoring, and more.
