24/7 Heavy-Duty CNC Breakdown Support – Fast, Reliable & On-Site

Heavy-Duty CNC Breakdown Support

Heavy-duty CNC machines are industrial-grade computer-controlled machines designed to handle high-load, high-precision machining tasks on large or tough materials. These machines are built for durability, rigidity, and prolonged operation in demanding environments.

Rapid‑Response Repair Workflow

  • 24/7 Hotline & Triage : Immediate phone or live‑chat intake to capture machine model, error codes, and site conditions.
  • Remote Diagnostics :Use VPN‑enabled PLC/HMI access or fault‑code analysis to pinpoint issues before dispatch.
  • Field Deployment :Dispatch mobile team with pre‑staged spares (drives, motors, bearings) and diagnostic tools.
  • On‑Site Repair : Mechanical teardown, component replacement or repair, re‑alignment, and balancing. Electrical troubleshooting: multimeter/oscilloscope checks, wiring repairs, PLC reloads. Hydraulic/pneumatic system leak testing and filter replacements.
  • Functional Testing & Job Proving : Run test parts under full load to verify accuracy, surface finish, and cycle times.
  • Handover & Documentation :Provide “as‑repaired” report, updated wiring diagrams, ladder‑logic backup, and recommended preventive actions.

Preventive & Predictive Maintenance

  • Scheduled Inspections: Monthly checklists covering lubrication, backlash, coolant quality, and electrical connectors.
  • Vibration Analysis:Quarterly spindle and bearing vibration monitoring to catch wear early.
  • Thermal Imaging: Identify hot spots in electrical cabinets or drive racks before failure.
  • IoT‑Enabled Monitoring: Real‑time telemetry of axis loads, motor currents, and coolant flow to trigger alerts.
