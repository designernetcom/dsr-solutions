About us

Trusted Marketplace for Used Machinery Solutions

DSR Solutions synonymous with excellence in precision engineering is a multi-product and services company established in September 2015. Built on a strong foundation of technical know-how acquired from world leaders in machine tools such as Empire Machine Tool and Juristic Machine Tool Pioneering the concept of CNC machine technology in India DSR solutions has successfully worked on Projects, Heavy Duty CNC Machine Installation and Commissioning, CNC Machine Maintenance, Servicing, Electrical and Mechanical material procurement, CNC machine sales, servicing and Marketing for the business development and CNC/Factory Automation.
